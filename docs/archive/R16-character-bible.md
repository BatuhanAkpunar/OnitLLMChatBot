# R16 — Character Bible: "The Onit Crew"

Onit is an AI product team you *lead* — so it should feel like choosing a roster
in a 16-bit arcade fighter / RPG party. Users bond with people, not avatars. The
crew is five Turkish specialists with warm, distinct personalities and a
**signature gesture** that telegraphs their job and lets the user connect at a
glance. Art: authentic SNES fighting-game character-select portraits — bold
outlines, limited 16-color palette, chunky crisp pixels, dramatic cel shading,
each on a flat role-colored stage. Two frames each: a confident neutral idle and
the signature gesture (the "el hareketi").

| Role | Name | Color | Personality | Signature gesture (why it bonds) |
|------|------|-------|-------------|----------------------------------|
| Analyst | **Mert** | cobalt blue | Curious, sharp, asks the right question. The one who *gets* you. | Pushes glasses up and raises an index finger — the "aha, I see what you need" beat. |
| Product Manager | **Elif** | violet | Decisive, warm leader. Turns chaos into a plan. | A confident thumbs-up — "yes, this is the move." Reassures the user they're in good hands. |
| Project Manager | **Kerem** | emerald | Calm, dependable, never drops a ball. Mustache, rolled sleeves. | A steady OK hand-sign + nod — "got it, we're on track." |
| Product Designer | **Zeynep** | hot pink | Playful, stylish, makes things delightful. Headphones on. | A cheeky peace/victory sign + wink — "ta-da, watch this." |
| QA Engineer | **Can** | teal | Vigilant, sharp-eyed, breaks things before users do. | A sharp point at the viewer — "gotcha, found the bug." Playful challenge energy. |

**Consistency rule:** the gesture frame is generated by *editing the neutral
frame* (same face/outfit/palette/background), so each character stays identical
across both frames. Frames: `public/avatars/<key>.webp` and `<key>_wave.webp`.
